# Mark files or folders as docs, not trackers

claude-howto's 535 tutorial checkboxes count as open tasks: they crowd its Board and the hook's session-start list.
Let a file (frontmatter `pmdai: doc`) or a folder (a setting) opt out of tracker parsing.
